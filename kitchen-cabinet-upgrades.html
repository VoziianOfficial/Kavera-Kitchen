Provider questions

What to ask before comparing cabinet quotes.

Cabinet projects can look simple from the outside, but the details matter. Use these prompts when reviewing independent provider conversations.

01

Replacement or refacing

Ask whether the provider focuses on replacement, refacing, refinishing, or hardware-only cabinet updates.

02

Door style and finish

Compare how providers explain cabinet door profiles, paint or stain finishes, durability, and sample review.

03

Storage and organization

Ask about drawers, pull-outs, pantry inserts, corner storage, vertical dividers, and built-in organization.

04

Hardware coordination

Review handle placement, finish compatibility, hinge options, soft-close details, and replacement compatibility.

05

Measurements and timing

Ask how measurements, ordering, site visits, removals, access, and installation scheduling are handled.

06

Verification

Verify licensing, insurance, warranty terms, material responsibility, and final quote details directly.
