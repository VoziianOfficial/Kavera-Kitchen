01 / Overview

How this Cookie Policy applies.

This Cookie Policy applies to the website operated by Kavera. The website helps homeowners compare independent kitchen remodeling provider options and request provider information.

Cookies and similar technologies may be used to make the website work properly, remember privacy choices, understand site usage, improve performance, and support security or operational needs.

Platform notice

Kavera is an independent provider-matching platform and is not a kitchen remodeling contractor. Provider availability, scope, licensing, insurance, estimates, timelines, warranties, and project terms must be verified directly with each independent provider.

02 / Cookies

What cookies and similar technologies are.

Cookies are small files or pieces of data that may be stored on your browser or device. Similar technologies can include localStorage, sessionStorage, pixels, tags, scripts, or browser-based identifiers.

These technologies can help websites remember choices, support functionality, measure performance, and understand general usage patterns.

  • Cookies may be set by the website or by third-party tools used on the website.
  • LocalStorage may remember your cookie banner choice.
  • Browser settings may allow you to block, delete, or manage cookies.
03 / Types

Types of cookies or storage Kavera may use.

Kavera may use different categories of cookies or similar technologies depending on website functionality and operational needs.

Essential functionality

Essential technologies support basic website operation, navigation, security, accessibility, and preference storage.

Preference storage

Preference storage may remember whether you accepted or declined the policy banner. The configured storage key may be controlled through the site configuration file.

Analytics and performance

Analytics or performance technologies may help Kavera understand general site usage, page performance, navigation patterns, and interface issues.

Communication and request support

Some technologies may support contact links, request form behavior, basic validation, or platform communications.

04 / Browser storage

How the policy banner stores your choice.

The Kavera policy banner appears at the bottom of the website until a visitor accepts or declines. The selected choice may be stored in the browser using localStorage.

This helps prevent the banner from appearing repeatedly after a choice has been made. If browser storage is cleared, the banner may appear again.

  • The banner includes Accept and Decline buttons.
  • The banner includes links to Privacy Policy, Cookie Policy, and Terms of Service.
  • The choice is stored locally in the browser where possible.
  • The banner should remain compact and should not block important navigation.

The policy choice does not create a contractor relationship, hire a provider, or affect the independent provider verification process.

05 / Choices

Your cookie choices.

You can manage cookies and similar technologies in several ways depending on your browser, device, and applicable privacy settings.

  • You may accept or decline the Kavera policy banner.
  • You may clear browser storage to remove saved localStorage choices.
  • You may block or delete cookies using browser settings.
  • You may use browser privacy controls or extensions where available.
  • You may contact Kavera with questions about this Cookie Policy.

Some website functionality may not work as expected if browser storage or cookies are disabled entirely.
