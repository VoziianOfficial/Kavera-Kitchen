Kavera is an independent provider-matching platform and does not perform kitchen remodeling work directly.

01 / Overview

How this policy applies.

This Privacy Policy applies to information collected through this website operated by Kavera. The site helps homeowners compare kitchen remodeling provider options and request information related to independent local companies.

Kavera is not a kitchen remodeling contractor, does not perform remodeling work, does not employ remodeling crews, and does not control the services, estimates, warranties, timelines, or work quality of independent providers.

Platform notice

Kavera is an independent provider-matching platform and is not a kitchen remodeling contractor. Provider availability, scope, licensing, insurance, estimates, timelines, warranties, and project terms must be verified directly with each independent provider.

02 / Information collected

Information Kavera may collect.

When you use this website, Kavera may collect information that you choose to provide, information collected automatically through site technologies, and information needed to operate the provider-matching experience.

Information you may provide

  • Full name.
  • Phone number.
  • Email address.
  • Selected kitchen remodeling service category.
  • General communication details if you contact Kavera by phone or email.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and browser information.
  • Approximate usage activity on the website.
  • Pages viewed, links clicked, and general site interaction data.
  • Cookie and similar technology preferences.

Kavera does not require a detailed project message, budget field, ZIP code field, or long questionnaire in the website request form.

03 / Use of information

How information may be used.

Kavera may use collected information to operate the website, respond to contact requests, help identify relevant service categories, improve site experience, maintain security, and communicate about provider-matching requests.

  • To process a request for kitchen provider options.
  • To help route the selected service category appropriately.
  • To communicate with users who contact the platform.
  • To maintain website functionality, security, and performance.
  • To understand how visitors use the website and improve usability.
  • To comply with applicable legal, regulatory, or operational obligations.

Submitting information through the website does not hire a contractor, create a contractor-client relationship, or guarantee provider availability, pricing, timelines, or project outcomes.

04 / Sharing

When information may be shared.

Kavera may share information with independent providers, service partners, vendors, or operational support providers when reasonably necessary to operate the website and provider-matching process.

  • With independent kitchen remodeling providers that may respond to a selected category.
  • With technology vendors that support hosting, analytics, security, or communication tools.
  • When required to comply with legal obligations or protect website rights and security.
  • In connection with a business transfer, restructuring, or similar platform-level event.

Independent providers are separate businesses. Their privacy practices, estimates, project terms, warranties, and communications should be reviewed directly with them.

05 / Cookies

Cookies and similar technologies.

Kavera may use cookies and similar technologies to support website functionality, remember privacy preferences, understand site usage, and improve the visitor experience.

You can accept or decline the policy banner when it appears. Your choice may be stored in your browser using localStorage or similar browser-based storage.

  • Essential cookies or storage may support basic website functions.
  • Preference storage may remember your privacy banner choice.
  • Analytics or usage tools may help understand website performance.

For more details, review the Cookie Policy.

06 / Choices

Your privacy choices.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights related to accessing, correcting, deleting, or limiting the use of certain personal information.

  • You may choose not to submit the request form.
  • You may contact Kavera to ask about information associated with your request.
  • You may adjust browser settings to manage cookies or similar technologies.
  • You may contact independent providers directly about information they collect or use.

Some information may be retained when needed for security, legal compliance, operational recordkeeping, dispute resolution, or platform protection.
